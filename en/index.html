Your farming partner in Bamako

SOFAB, Société Falaise Agro Business, distributes farm inputs and supports farmers so that farming becomes simpler, more modern and more profitable.

At our shop in Sotuba ACI you will find vegetable and forage seeds, fertilisers, crop protection products, equipment and chemicals. With every product, we give you the advice that goes with it.

We also disinfect premises, offices and homes.

Our services
